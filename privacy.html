Privacy policy.

Business Shorts Scheduler is a private tool used to upload prepared videos to an authorized YouTube channel.

Information used

When the channel owner connects a Google account, the app receives an OAuth refresh token with permission to upload YouTube videos. The app uses the token to request short lived access tokens and upload videos and their metadata. It does not request a Google password.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth client credentials and refresh token are stored as secrets in a private GitHub repository used for automation. Prepared video files, titles, descriptions, and scheduled times are processed by GitHub Actions and sent to YouTube. We do not sell Google user data or share the OAuth token with advertisers.

Retention and control

The channel owner can revoke the app's access in their Google Account connections. We can also remove the stored GitHub secrets and stop the automation on request. Uploaded videos remain on YouTube until the channel owner removes them there.

This website

This static website does not ask visitors to sign in and does not run our own analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub hosts the pages and may process technical access data under its own privacy statement.
